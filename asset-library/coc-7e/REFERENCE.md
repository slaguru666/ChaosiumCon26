# Call of Cthulhu — 7th Edition Reference
*Chaosium Inc. — For use in scenario mechanics and stat blocks*

---

## Characteristics

| Characteristic | Abbreviation | Derivation |
|---------------|--------------|------------|
| Strength | STR | 3D6 × 5 |
| Constitution | CON | 3D6 × 5 |
| Size | SIZ | (2D6+6) × 5 |
| Dexterity | DEX | 3D6 × 5 |
| Appearance | APP | 3D6 × 5 |
| Intelligence | INT | (2D6+6) × 5 |
| Power | POW | 3D6 × 5 |
| Education | EDU | (2D6+6) × 5 |
| Luck | — | 3D6 × 5 |

**Hit Points:** (CON + SIZ) / 10 (round down)
**Magic Points:** POW / 5
**Sanity:** POW (starting max 99)
**Movement:** 8 (human, standard)

---

## Skill Rolls

| Difficulty | Roll Required |
|------------|---------------|
| Regular | Equal to or under skill value |
| Hard | Half skill value |
| Extreme | One-fifth skill value |
| Automatic | No roll needed |

**Opposed Rolls:** Both roll; higher success level wins. Ties go to higher skill.
**Pushed Rolls:** Re-roll once after failure — consequences on second failure.
**Bonus Die:** Roll 2 × tens dice, take lower.
**Penalty Die:** Roll 2 × tens dice, take higher.

---

## Combat

**Attack sequence:** DEX order (highest first)
**Fighting (Brawl):** Base 25%
**Firearms:** Vary by weapon
**Dodge:** Half DEX

**Damage Bonus (DB):**
| STR + SIZ | Damage Bonus |
|-----------|-------------|
| 2–64 | -2 |
| 65–84 | -1 |
| 85–124 | None |
| 125–164 | +1D4 |
| 165–204 | +1D6 |

**Major Wound:** Damage ≥ half max HP in one hit — CON roll or fall unconscious
**Death:** HP reaches 0 or below

---

## Sanity

**Sanity Loss:** Listed as X/YDZ (e.g., 1/1D6 — 1 on success, 1D6 on failure)
**Temporary Insanity:** Lose 5+ SAN in one roll, or 10+ in one scene
**Indefinite Insanity:** Lose 20% of current SAN within one session
**Permanent Insanity:** SAN reaches 0

**Bout of Madness (Temporary):**
Roll on Bout of Madness table or GM choice:
Amnesia | Psychosomatic Blindness/Deafness | Violence | Paranoia | Hallucinations | Flee in panic | Faint | Hysteria

## Era Notes
- **1920s:** No mobile phones, limited forensics, telegram/post communication, social class matters
- **Modern:** Internet, mobile phones, CCTV, DNA evidence — investigators can look things up quickly
