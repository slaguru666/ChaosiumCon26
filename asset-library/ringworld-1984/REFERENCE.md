# Ringworld — 1984 Edition Reference
*Chaosium Inc. — Based on Larry Niven's Known Space*

---

## Characteristics

| Characteristic | Abbreviation | Roll |
|---------------|--------------|------|
| Strength | STR | 3D6 |
| Constitution | CON | 3D6 |
| Size | SIZ | 2D6+6 |
| Intelligence | INT | 2D6+6 |
| Power | POW | 3D6 |
| Dexterity | DEX | 3D6 |
| Appearance | APP | 3D6 |
| Education | EDU | 3D6+3 |

**Hit Points:** (CON + SIZ) / 2 (round up)
**Move:** 10 (Human standard in 1G)
**Perception:** INT + POW *(used for awareness/alertness)*

---

## Skill Rolls

**Standard:** D100 equal to or under skill %
**Difficulty modifiers** apply as circumstance dictates (GM judgement)
**Opposed:** Compare success levels; ties go to higher skill

---

## Combat

**Initiative:** DEX rank
**Attacks per round:** Typically 1 (2 for high DEX with training)
**Ranged combat:** Range modifiers apply; cover matters

**Damage Modifier:**
| STR + SIZ | DB |
|-----------|----|
| 2–12 | -1D6 |
| 13–16 | -1D4 |
| 17–24 | None |
| 25–32 | +1D4 |
| 33–40 | +1D6 |

**Advanced Technology:** Weapons in Known Space are often far beyond conventional firearms
- **Flashlight-laser:** 2D6+2, burns, ignites materials
- **Variable sword:** 1D6+4+DB, cuts through most materials
- **Slaver Stasis Field:** Freezes target completely (no save if hit)

## The Ringworld — Key Facts
- **Diameter:** ~1 million miles
- **Circumference:** ~3 million miles
- **Width:** ~997,000 miles
- **Surface area:** ~3 million Earths
- **Spin gravity:** ~0.992 G
- **Day/night:** Shadow squares on wire
- **Rim walls:** 1000 miles high, retain atmosphere
- **Built by:** The Pak Protectors (ancient ancestors of humanity)
- **Current state:** Largely inhabited by devolved hominids; ancient tech ruins everywhere

## Setting Notes — The Ringworld
- Scale is vast — travel between locations is a campaign unto itself
- Ancient technology is everywhere; most of it is incomprehensible or inert
- The Ringworld is failing — systems degrading over millennia
- Hominid species vary enormously; most have no concept of the structure they live on
- Known Space context: Puppeteers have fled, Kzin are a recent defeated threat, ARM (UN police) operate in the background
- Tone: Wonder mixed with danger — the Ringworld rewards curiosity and punishes arrogance
