# Stormbringer — 1st Edition Reference
*Chaosium Inc. — Michael Moorcock's Young Kingdoms*

---

## Characteristics

| Characteristic | Abbreviation | Roll |
|---------------|--------------|------|
| Strength | STR | 3D6 |
| Constitution | CON | 3D6 |
| Size | SIZ | 2D6+6 |
| Intelligence | INT | 2D6+6 |
| Power | POW | 3D6 |
| Dexterity | DEX | 3D6 |
| Charisma | CHA | 3D6 |

**Hit Points:** CON + SIZ (combined, not divided)
**Magic Points:** POW

---

## Skill Rolls

**Base mechanic:** D100 roll equal to or under skill %
**Resistance Table:** Used for opposed actions (STR vs STR, POW vs POW, etc.)
- Find the active characteristic on the column, passive on the row
- Result is the % chance of success

---

## Combat

**Strike Rank:** DEX + SIZ (higher acts first)
**Weapon Attack:** D100 vs weapon skill %
**Parry:** D100 vs parry skill %
**Dodge:** DEX × 3%

**Combat Resolution:**
- Attack success + Parry failure = full damage
- Attack success + Parry success = half damage
- Special success (critical): impale/slash/crush depending on weapon type

**Damage Modifier:**
| STR + SIZ | Damage Mod |
|-----------|-----------|
| 2–12 | -1D4 |
| 13–16 | -1D2 |
| 17–24 | +0 |
| 25–32 | +1D4 |
| 33–40 | +1D6 |

---

## Allegiance System
*Central to Stormbringer — tracks alignment to Law, Chaos, or Balance*

| Allegiance | Gain Condition | Loss Condition |
|-----------|----------------|----------------|
| Law | Orderly, lawful acts | Chaotic, destructive acts |
| Chaos | Chaotic, destructive acts | Orderly, constructive acts |
| Balance | Balanced, neutral acts | Extreme acts either way |

**High Allegiance:** May attract attention of Lords of Law/Chaos — boons or curses
**Patron Demon/Lord:** At high allegiance, may bind a supernatural patron

---

## Demons & Summoning
*Key mechanic unique to Stormbringer*

**Summoning:** POW vs POW on Resistance Table
**Binding:** Extended ritual, POW cost
**Demon Types:** Combat, Intellect, Beast, Guardian, Power

**Demon Attributes:** Vary by type; GMs assign STR, CON, SIZ, INT, POW, DEX, CHA + special powers

## Setting Notes — The Young Kingdoms
- Sword and sorcery; death is common and meaningful
- Magic is dangerous — demons and Lords of Chaos/Law take notice
- Allegiance shapes the world's reaction to characters
- Cities: Imrryr (ruined), Karlaak, Pan Tang, Nadsokor
- Key NPCs from fiction should be used sparingly — this is the players' story
