Core Mechanics

Percentile Resolution

Roll 1D100. If the result is equal to or below the relevant skill, characteristic multiple, or resistance chance, the action succeeds. Higher is generally worse.

Critical Successes

A critical success occurs when the roll is equal to or below one tenth of the needed chance, rounded down.

Chance NeededCritical On
35%01–03
46%01–04
90%01–09

Fumbles

A roll of 00 is always a fumble. If the acting skill is below 50%, a roll of 99 is also a fumble.

Characteristic Rolls

When a raw characteristic directly matters, the GM calls for a roll against a multiple of it.

DifficultyRoll Against
Easy5 × characteristic
Normal3 × characteristic
Hard1 × characteristic

Resistance Formula

When two forces directly oppose each other (POW vs POW, STR vs SIZ, etc.):

Chance = 50 + (active value − resisting value) × 5

The active character succeeds if they roll equal to or below the chance. Minimum 5%, maximum 95%.

Improving Skills

After a meaningful success in play, at end of adventure roll 1D100, adding INT − 12 if INT is above 12. If the result exceeds the current skill, the skill rises by 1D10.

Training with a master improves a skill by 1D6 − 2 after one week of dedicated practice (minimum 0).

Characteristics

Roll 3D6 for each characteristic. If the total of all seven is below 64, you may reroll the entire set.

Hit Points

HP = CON + 1 per SIZ point above 12 − 1 per SIZ point below 9
(SIZ penalty cannot reduce HP below ½ CON)

At 0 HP or below, the character is dead. No resurrection in the Young Kingdoms.

Sanity

Starting SAN = POW × 5 (modified by nationality)
Temporary Madness Threshold (SFT) = ½ starting SAN (never changes)

SAN ranges 1–99. At 0 SAN the character is completely insane and passes to GM control.

Increasing Characteristics

Characteristics rarely increase. If the GM calls a characteristic roll and the character succeeds, they may attempt to increase it after the adventure: roll 2D6. On 7, the characteristic rises by 1. On 2, it falls by 1. All other results: no change.

Skills & Bonuses

Skills are percentages. Roll 1D100; equal to or below = success. Characters not knowing a skill attempt it at the relevant bonus; on success they acquire it at that value.

All characters begin with the following at 10% (+ bonus): Jump, Climb, Dodge, Balance, Persuade, Listen, Hide.

Skill Bonus Calculation

Average characteristics are 9–12. Each point above 12 usually adds +1%; each point below 9 subtracts −1%. SIZ is reversed for Parry, Agility, and Stealth bonuses.

Skill Categories

Weapon Skills

Attack and Parry are separate skills. Learning both costs two skill choices. Missile and thrown weapons normally require Attack only. Shields have Attack and Parry.

Knowledge Skills (require training)

Poison Lore, Plant Lore, and Music Lore cannot be improved through ordinary experience — they require a teacher or special study.

Educated Classes

Nobles, Merchants, and Priests may learn Speak and Read/Write for another language as a single skill choice. Other classes must learn them separately.

Combat

Combat Round (≈12 seconds)

  1. Declare intentions (lowest DEX may declare first so fastest can react).
  2. Resolve combat and missile fire in DEX order (highest first).
  3. Move unengaged characters; manifest summoned elementals/demons.

One attack per round normally. No human can be attacked by more than five opponents simultaneously.

Attacking

Roll against the weapon's Attack skill. On a hit, the defender chooses:

  1. Take the blow and rely on armor.
  2. Parry with weapon or shield.
  3. Dodge — only if they have not yet attacked this round.

Parrying

Roll against Parry skill. Success = no damage. Additional parries in the same round are at cumulative −20% each (−20%, −40%, −60%…). Only one defence per attack — cannot try shield and then weapon if the first fails.

Arrows and sling stones can only be parried by shields. Thrown weapons can be dodged or parried.

Dodge

  • Cannot Dodge if you have already attacked this round.
  • Cannot Dodge as a second attempt after a failed Parry.
  • A critical hit can only be Dodged by a critical Dodge.

Critical Hits

Roll equal to or below 1/10 of Attack skill.

  • Double normal damage.
  • Ignores all armor (including demon armor).
  • Forces Major Wound roll even if damage would be minor.
  • Target stunned for at least 5 minutes.
  • Parrying a critical hit breaks the parrying weapon/shield (Demon Weapons save on 1×CON or break).
  • Attacker may choose to disarm instead; a Parry prevents the disarm.

Critical Parries

Same condition (1/10 of Parry skill). A critical parry breaks any normal weapon that scored an ordinary hit. Against a critical hit, both weapons break.

Weapon Mastery (90%+ in both Attack and Parry)

  • May train others (1D6−2 skill per week; roll Attack and Parry separately).
  • After a successful Parry, may immediately make an extra riposte attack (at −20% if other attacks were made).

Surprise & Ambush

Roll Ambush skill. Success = 1D4−1 rounds of surprise. During surprise, victims cannot Attack, Parry, Dodge, or riposte. Armor still protects.

If Ambush fails, victims may roll See:

  • Success: may draw weapon/shield and parry this round; weapon masters may attack.
  • Failure: surprised 1 round (cannot parry or fight).
  • Round 2 after failure: may parry but not attack.
  • Round 3: normal combat.

Initiative (Sudden Attack)

Both sides roll 1D10 + DEX. If attacker wins, they may attack and defender can only Dodge. Normal DEX order resumes next round.

Falling into water or a soft surface: subtract 2D6 from damage. Armor does not protect against falls. Plate adds +1D6 extra damage.

Fire

Torch used as weapon: 1D6 damage. When touched by flame, roll 3×POW (5×POW in plate) or catch fire: 1D10 fire damage before extinguished. A major fire wound costs 1D10 CON (and equal HP) permanently; if CON reaches 0, death by secondary infection.

Disease

Exposure: roll 1D100 against 2×CON. Failure = diseased. Then roll 2×POW to recover. Mild failure: −1D4 CON temporarily. Severe failure: death. Recovering from severe disease costs 1D4 CON permanently but grants +1 POW.

Healing

First Aid

One attempt per wound situation. Success: stops bleeding, restores 1D3 HP. Critical success: restores 1D3+3 HP or prevents long-term major wound effect. Fumble: healer causes extra damage equal to half the patient's lost HP.

Natural Healing

Minor wounds: 1 HP/day of rest. Major wounds: require First Aid first, then 1 HP/week.

Magic & Sorcery

Mortals cannot directly create magic. All magic requires summoning, binding, bargaining with, or serving supernatural beings from other planes.

The sign of Chaos is the eight-pointed star (octagon). The sign of Law is the triangle. Chaotic demons are contained in octagons; Law rituals use triangles.

Advancing Sorcery Rank

Qualifying INT+POW is not enough — training is required:

  1. Grimoire study: 40 − INT months; must succeed at roll under 3×POW (failure = death).
  2. NPC teacher: 30 − INT months; GM sets payment and rank.
  3. PC teacher: 30 − INT months; neither character can do anything else during training.

Summoning a Lesser Elemental

  1. The appropriate element must be physically present.
  2. Concentrate and chant for 2D10 minutes uninterrupted.
  3. Roll the summoning skill.
  4. On success: elemental obeys one order, then is free unless bound.

Binding a Lesser Elemental

  1. Choose a receptacle object (ring, sword, stone, etc.).
  2. Roll under 3×POW.
  3. Success: elemental is bound; can be called up to 4 times per hour (4th call frees it).
  4. Failure: elemental freed; sorcerer's POW falls to 1, recovers at 1/hour.

Critical binding success: roll 1D4−2 POW gain/loss (positive = sorcerer takes from elemental; negative = elemental drains sorcerer).

Multiple bindings of same element: roll 2D6 and try to exceed the number of bound elementals of that type. Failure attracts the Elemental Lord's attention.

Elemental Lords

Cannot be controlled, only bargained with. Requires third-rank sorcery and the appropriate element to be present. The Lord sets its own price or service requirement.

Demons

Lesser demons are beings of Chaos from other planes, bound to the Lords of Chaos. They are more dangerous and flexible than elementals.

Summoning a Demon

Normal summoning: roll the summoning skill; takes hours. On success, bargain for one service or one day of aid.

Quick summoning: takes 2D10 minutes; use either 2×POW or half the summoning skill.

Binding a Demon

Resolve POW vs POW on the Resistance formula.

  • Success: demon becomes permanent servant; may be bound into an object.
  • Failure: sorcerer's POW falls to 1; permanently loses 1 POW; Combat and Possession Demons attack the summoner; all already-bound demons may rebel.
  • Critical success (demon POW ≥ summoner's): sorcerer gains 1D4−1 POW.

Summoning Beast Lords

Requires fourth-rank sorcery. Normally only Melniboneans may summon Beast Lords due to ancient pacts. Requires knowledge of a poetic rune specific to the beast kind. A non-Melnibonean may be taught one such rune by a Melnibonean sorcerer under extraordinary circumstances.

Summoning Lords of Law

  • INT + POW 48+
  • Stand in an equilateral triangle
  • Use ritual objects grouped in threes
  • Roll equal to or below INT + POW

Summoning Lords of Chaos

  • INT + POW 48+
  • Stand on the eight-arrow sign of Chaos
  • Offer human sacrifice
  • Roll equal to or below POW + number of sacrifices
Gods cannot be controlled. The GM plays them. They may demand CON, POW, CHA, worship, service, human sacrifice, or impossible deeds.

Cult Membership Levels

  • Worshipper: ordinary believer, performs rites. May belong to multiple non-opposing cults.
  • Priest: cult functionary; leads services; has direct relationship with the god; may hold temple rank.
  • Agent: soul dedicated to one deity; can call divine intervention.

Elan

Elan measures divine favour (0–99). Gained by performing actions appropriate to the deity.

50 Elan → +1 POW (convert at any time)
If Elan reaches 100: must convert at least 50 immediately
Effective maximum: 99

Calling for Divine Intervention

  1. Roll 1D100.
  2. If result > Elan: call fails, Elan is halved.
  3. If result ≤ Elan: call succeeds, subtract the roll from Elan.
  4. GM grants minimum effective version of the request.
Use divine intervention only in desperate moments, not for convenience.

Becoming an Agent

  1. Visit a temple of the chosen deity.
  2. Permanently sacrifice any number of POW points (cannot reduce POW below 3).
  3. Add sacrificed POW to CHA.
  4. Roll 1D100 equal to or below CHA + sacrificed POW.

Success = Agent status + personalized amulet. Failure = POW still lost, cannot try that deity again. One attempt per session; one deity only.

Sanity

Make SAN rolls for supernatural shock — not for ordinary danger or everyday violence. Stormbringer characters are hardened warriors; SAN is reserved for Chaos, demons, and gods.

Roll 1D100 against current SAN. Success = smaller or no loss. Failure = listed loss (usually permanent).

If SAN loss within 10 minutes (50 rounds) equals or exceeds the SFT (½ starting SAN), the character suffers Temporary Madness.

Recovering SAN

Characters regain SAN by defeating or frustrating a god's plans: banishing a god ≈ +30 SAN; thwarting divine plans ≈ +20 SAN. SAN can never exceed its original starting value. All recovery is at GM discretion.

Curing complete insanity (SAN 0) requires divine intervention — and gods may prefer the character stay mad.

Character Creation Checklist

  1. Roll STR, CON, SIZ, INT, POW, DEX, CHA on 3D6 each.
  2. If total is below 64, reroll the whole set.
  3. Roll or choose nationality; apply modifiers.
  4. Calculate Hit Points (CON ± SIZ modifier).
  5. Calculate starting SAN (POW × 5, modified by nationality) and SFT (½ SAN).
  6. Roll or choose Social Origin; record class skills, money, and equipment.
  7. Choose 1D6 + 2 additional skills (weapon Attack + Parry = 2 choices each).
  8. Calculate all Skill Bonuses from characteristics.
  9. Choose weapons within STR/DEX requirements.
  10. If INT 16+ and INT + POW 32+, decide on sorcery access and roll/choose elemental type.
  11. If Priest or Agent, record cult, starting Elan (Priests get 2D6), taboos, and amulet.
  12. Optionally roll on Personal History table (1D100).
  13. Give the character a reason to travel with the group.
Characters begin at age 25 by default. Each year below 25: −5% per class skill. Each year above 25: +1% (ordinary), +2% (Noble), or +3% (Priest) to Knowledge skills.

GM Guidance

Principles

  1. Combat is decisive. A single critical hit can change or end a life.
  2. Magic is never casual. Every summoning creates obligation, risk, or debt.
  3. Law and Chaos are not Good and Evil. Both become monstrous when absolute.
  4. Characters need motives. Loyalty, revenge, faith, ambition, curiosity, love, debt, exile, doom.
  5. Keep Elric in the background. Player characters should be the centre of their own tragedy.
