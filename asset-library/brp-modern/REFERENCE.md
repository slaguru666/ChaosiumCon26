# Basic Role-Playing — Modern Edition Reference
*Chaosium Inc. — Universal Game Engine*

---

## Characteristics

| Characteristic | Abbreviation | Roll |
|---------------|--------------|------|
| Strength | STR | 3D6 |
| Constitution | CON | 3D6 |
| Size | SIZ | 2D6+6 |
| Intelligence | INT | 2D6+6 |
| Power | POW | 3D6 |
| Dexterity | DEX | 3D6 |
| Appearance | APP | 3D6 |
| Education | EDU | 3D6+3 *(optional)* |

**Hit Points:** (CON + SIZ) / 2 (round up)
**Power Points:** POW (recovers 1/hour)
**Characteristic Rolls:** Roll D100 under (Characteristic × 5)

---

## Skill Rolls

**Standard Roll:** D100 equal to or under skill %
**Difficulty:**
| Level | Modifier |
|-------|----------|
| Easy | × 2 |
| Standard | × 1 |
| Difficult | × 1/2 |
| Very Difficult | × 1/5 |

**Opposed Rolls:** Compare levels of success; highest wins
**Critical Success:** 01–05 or 1/10 of skill (lower)
**Fumble:** 96–00 or above 30 when skill is under 30

---

## Success Levels
1. Critical Success
2. Special Success (roll ≤ 1/5 skill)
3. Success
4. Failure
5. Fumble

---

## Combat

**Strike Rank:** DEX rank + SIZ rank (higher goes first)
**Combat Actions per round:** Varies by DEX
**Parry:** Active defence with weapon or shield
**Dodge:** Active defence — DEX × 5% base

**Damage Modifier:**
| STR + SIZ | Damage Mod |
|-----------|-----------|
| 2–12 | -1D6 |
| 13–16 | -1D4 |
| 17–24 | None |
| 25–32 | +1D4 |
| 33–40 | +1D6 |

**Hit Locations (optional):** D20 roll determines location
**Major Wound:** Damage ≥ half max HP — special effect applies
**Incapacitation:** HP reaches 2 or below
**Death:** HP 0 or negative

## Powers (if used)
- **Magic:** Power Point cost, POW vs POW resistance
- **Psychic:** Concentration required, Power Point cost
- **Super Powers:** Campaign-level decision

## Setting Notes — Modern Urban / Action-Horror
- Firearms common; combat is fast and lethal
- Corporate structures, surveillance, encrypted comms
- Medical facilities, forensics, emergency services are accessible
- Power Points model stamina/resolve as well as literal power
