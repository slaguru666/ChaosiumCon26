# Midjourney Prompts — Stormbringer RPG (French Oriflam 1984 / Young Kingdoms)

Dark sword and sorcery. Michael Moorcock's Young Kingdoms: a world of decaying empire, ancient pacts with demons, and the eternal struggle of Law versus Chaos. Art direction: 1970s–80s fantasy illustration — Michael Whelan, Chris Achilleos, John Blanche. Rich jewel tones bleeding into deep shadow. Ornate detail. Decay under beauty.

**Global style anchor (add to any prompt):**
`Stormbringer RPG Young Kingdoms dark fantasy illustration, 1970s sword and sorcery art, rich jewel tones and deep shadow, decadent ancient empire, Michael Whelan style painterly, occult atmosphere`
