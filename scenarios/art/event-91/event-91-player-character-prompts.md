# Event 91 — The Night Crawler | Player Character Portrait Prompts

**Art Direction:** Neo-noir corporate dystopia, 2087. These are portraits for the player character sheets — they sit in the PDF alongside stats, skills and HP tracker. The aesthetic is painterly realism: detailed faces, specific augments visible but understated, lighting from neon or rain-reflected sources. Think the character design work from films like *Blade Runner 2049* or *Collateral* — human, grounded, specific. Not anime. Not mass-effect power armour. Not neon-overload.

**Consistent style tags (apply to all):**
All portraits at `--ar 2:3`. Style: cinematic neo-noir character portrait, 2087 corporate dystopia, painterly digital oil painting, high contrast, rain-slicked urban atmosphere, realistic human proportions, dramatic under-lighting or neon-ambient, NOT anime, NOT generic sci-fi, NOT power armour, NOT neon-overload aesthetic, subtle augments, Michael Mann Collateral aesthetic, gritty photorealistic illustration

**Workflow:**
Generate at `--ar 2:3`. Upscale the best result. Crop to head-and-shoulders for the PDF header banner (approx portrait-width by 52pt height at 300dpi). Full portrait usable as a separate player handout.

**Drift correction:** If Midjourney goes too glossy or generic, add: `NOT Cyberpunk 2077 video game art, NOT Mass Effect, NOT Overwatch, grounded realistic dystopia, film noir portrait photography translated to painting`

## Usage Notes

- All six at `--ar 2:3`. Upscale before using in PDF.
- For PDF header banner (52pt height): crop tightly to face and upper chest.
- For player handout: use full portrait.
- **Swap into PDF:** In `build_brp_characters_v2.py`, replace `PortraitPlaceholder(...)` with `RLImage('/path/to/image.jpg', width=port_w, height=port_h)` in the build loop. Match character to portrait by `char['name']` exactly as in the `PORTRAITS` dict pattern from the Stormbringer script.
- **Style drift correction:** Add `NOT Cyberpunk 2077 video game art, NOT Mass Effect, film noir portrait photography translated to painting` if results are too glossy or generic.
- **Consistency across all six:** If the first portrait lands well, use `--sref [job-id]` on subsequent generations to pull the painterly style across the set.
