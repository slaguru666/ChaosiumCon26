# Event 96 — Is Love the Answer? | Midjourney Art Prompts

**Style Foundation (apply to all):**
All artwork should be hand-drawn ink sketch and pencil illustration style.
Clean, organic linework. Not photorealistic. Simple, gestural, suggestive rather than detailed.
Warm tones for the Ringworld surface and organic subjects.
Heavy shadow and precise angular linework for the antagonist.
Vast spaces implied rather than fully rendered.

---

## 96-ART-01 — Cover Illustration

```
Two silhouettes standing on a flat alien plain, one in practical off-world survey gear, one in layered fabric garments, both looking toward an impossible horizon. The horizon curves upward in both directions into sky, the arc of a structure too vast to comprehend. A single tall spire rises behind them in the far distance. The figures are small against the immensity. No faces visible. Hand-drawn ink sketch style, minimal detail, gestural organic linework, pencil shading on the horizon curve, wide landscape composition, warm tones, science fiction 1984 paperback illustration style --ar 3:4 --style raw
```

## 96-ART-03 — Serenthis (Portrait)

```
Portrait of a young woman of striking appearance, dark skin, close-cut hair with a single braid from temple to shoulder, wearing layered fabric garments in grey-green tones. At both wrists, intricately woven bands. Her hands are open, partially visible in the lower frame. Expression attentive and direct — not guarded, not welcoming, something more precise than either. She is listening like she is taking notes. Ink and pencil portrait, detailed linework on face and hands, softer on fabric, warm tones, science fiction illustration style, 1984 paperback art aesthetic --ar 3:4 --style raw
```

## 96-ART-04 — Ghresh-Ka (The Warlord)

```
A massive alien warrior, feline species, nearly three metres tall, heavily muscled, orange-cream fur, wearing battle-worn armour adapted from salvaged materials. One ear partially missing. He stands still, not threatening — deciding. His amber eyes are calm and calculating. Heavy shadow dominates the lower half of the composition. He fills most of the frame. The expression is patient, which is worse than angry. Ink sketch, heavy shadow, precise angular linework for armour, softer lines for fur, science fiction 1984 illustration style, menacing through stillness --ar 3:4 --style raw
```

## 96-ART-06 — The Storm Wall

```
A vast alien plain stretching to a horizon that curves gently upward. In the middle distance, a circular settlement with a spire. On the horizon in every direction, a massive weather system building — but wrong, too geometric, too structured to be natural. The storm wall is rendered with dense parallel horizontal lines suggesting an engineered atmospheric event. Above it, vast rectangular shadow panels are shifting against the sky. Ink sketch, wide panoramic composition, settlement small in frame, storm dominant on horizon, structured geometry for the storm, organic detail in the foreground --ar 16:9 --style raw
```

## USAGE NOTES

- All prompts are for Midjourney v6 or later
- Add `--no photorealistic, --no 3D render, --no digital art` to any that skew too polished
- If the output is too dark: add `warm light, golden hour lighting` to the prompt
- If Ghresh-Ka looks too cartoonish: add `realistic anatomy, detailed fur texture, no exaggeration`
- If the market feels too empty: add `bustling crowd, dense composition, overlapping figures`
- The cover (ART-01) and choice (ART-07) are the most important — prioritise these first
- Once generated, provide the images back to Claude and they will be embedded in the GM app
