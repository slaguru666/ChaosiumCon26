## KEEPER SYNOPSIS

New York City, 1979. The city is broke, burning, and on the edge. Midnight at Van Cortlandt Park in the Bronx: the most ambitious gang summit in the city's history. Cyrus, war chief of the Gramercy Riffs, has called every set in the five boroughs to a single meeting with a single message — peace, unity, and enough collective muscle to own the city.

Then someone shoots Cyrus dead, and someone else screams that the Coney Island Warriors did it.

The investigators are the Warriors' delegation: six gang members, unarmed by summit rules, in hostile territory with every set in the city hunting them. Their only path home is south through the length of Manhattan and across to Brooklyn — by subway, on foot, and by whatever they can manage.

**KEEPER TRUTH:** Lester Creed, vessel of the entity known as the Black Conductor, orchestrated the murder. Cyrus had discovered what the Third Rail Choir was doing beneath the city and was secretly building a cross-gang coalition to stop it. The Choir moved first. The Warriors were framed because Cyrus pressed a brass token — a ward-seal that fits the Stillwell Avenue breaker shrine — into one of their hands as he died. Without that token, the gate opens at dawn.

**THE ENTITY:** Nyarlathotep, manifest here as the Black Conductor — an ancient transit intelligence that has ridden the city's rail infrastructure since before the first IRT line was laid. It does not want to destroy New York. It wants to *run* it. The subway grid was its prison. Cyrus understood that. The ward network across the entire subway system was its cage. The cage is crumbling.

**DEADLINE:** 6:00 AM. After that, containment only. After 7:00 AM — no known procedure.

## SCENARIO OUTLINE

### INTRODUCTION — VAN CORTLANDT PARK (20 min)

Midnight at Van Cortlandt Park. Cyrus addresses the assembled gangs. The shot comes without warning. Someone screams the Warriors did it. The floodlights die the instant the sirens start.

**Spot Hidden:** During Cyrus's speech, inner-ring Riffs make an odd repeated gesture — three fingers curled, medallion touched to forehead. Log who notices.

**The Token:** One investigator can reach Cyrus before the crowd closes. He says once: *"Don't let it reach the end of the line."* He presses the brass token into their palm. It is warm. It stays warm.

**Creed in the Crowd:** One investigator glimpses a face moving calmly against the fleeing bodies. Watching with mild professional interest. Gone before it can be studied.

Drive the investigators out of the park immediately. No deliberation. The city closes the moment Cyrus falls.

---

### ACT 1 — THE BRONX RUN (~50 min)

**1-1: Cemetery Rail Cut (~10 min)**

Fastest route south. A Riffs lookout dead by a retaining wall — no wound, no bruising. Eyes burned flat silver. Not reflective. Silver, like old cutlery. He sat down and stopped.

- **Medicine / First Aid (Doc auto):** No cause. Core temp unexpectedly cold.
- **Occult (Hard):** Seated position matches voluntary consciousness-transfer postures in obscure esoteric texts.
- **Spot Hidden:** Matchbook under a rail tie. Spiral over three parallel lines — the Choir sigil. Spray recognises it.

*Clues: lookout's notebook (H2), Third Rail Choir matchbook*
*SAN: Examining the silver eyes — 0/1*

---

**1-2: The Witness (~8 min)**

Young man in a stairwell. He saw the shooter. Small, pale, not Warriors' colours. Already moving toward the exit before Cyrus fell. Lips moving — not shouting — quiet, like counting. He looked like someone arriving on schedule.

- **Fast Talk (Normal):** Confirms the Warriors didn't fire.
- **Intimidate (Normal):** Adds: "His eyes didn't look right in the dark."

*Clues: witness description (H6)*

---

**1-3: Gun Hill Butchers (~10 min)**

Twelve Butchers running the citywide bounty. Bats, pipes, at least one firearm.

Run / Fight / Fast Talk (Hard if recognised) / Split and Stealth. Injuries expected. Deaths are not — Act 1.

---

**1-4: Transit Sweep (~10 min)**

Doyle's unit at the concourse. Wheels routes through service access.

**First Mythos Intrusion:** Whoever carries the token notices the train windows reflected in dark tunnel glass show passengers — full cars, nobody moving. Train arrives. Doors open. Empty.

Token-holder gets a fragment: tall figure in conductor's uniform, face a featureless pale oval, punching tickets on material that is not paper. Less than a second. Does not repeat.

*SAN: Platform reflection passengers — 0/1 · Token vision — 1/1D4*

---

**1-5: First Train South (~12 min)**

Breathing room. PA carries something like a broadcast, then cuts to rhythmic counting that stops when anyone focuses on it. The car immediately ahead stays dark and empty for the entire Bronx run. Let investigators compare notes. Doc's note should surface here.

*Clues: shell casing (if park was searched)*

---

### ACT 2 — THE MANHATTAN TRAP (~75 min)

**2-1: 96th Street Station (~20 min)**

Velvet Static's broadcast. Doyle's cordon. The table splits.

**Group A (Wheels leads):** Service tunnel. Walls freshly chalked with geometric patterns. Binding wards.
- **Occult (Normal):** Binding wards. Several broken — Cyrus's death shattered the primary. The secondary is crumbling.
- **Cthulhu Mythos (Hard):** Notation found in no published texts. Sealed before the first tunnel was dug.

**Group B (Swan or Doc leads):** Velvet Lizzies. Dolores leads — operating from fear.
- **Psychology (Hard):** She can be reached.
- **Persuade / Fast Talk (Normal if Psych passed):** "Cyrus was building an army against the Choir. Someone found out and moved first."

*Clues: ward network (wards_discovery), Dolores's testimony*

---

**2-2: The Binding Wards (layered)**

Chalk markings recur through Act 2 in every tunnel. Cumulative: the marks form a city-wide containment structure. The entire subway grid converted over decades by multiple people who may not have known they were part of the same effort. Failing from north to south since midnight.

*Wheels: the ward placements required deep MTA access. He knew this system. He never saw who did this.*

---

**2-3: The Broadcast (~8 min)**

Accurate to their current location. Describes them in real-time.

**Listen (Hard):** Second voice beneath Sandy's: low, uninflected, counting. Stations. Distances. Then the investigators' real names — not street names. Birth certificate names.

*Use the real names from the character sheets. Two encounters maximum.*
*SAN: Real names in the broadcast — 1/1D4*

---

**2-4: The Dark Ride (~10 min)**

Train stops between stations. Lights fail. Emergency lighting: red-orange, dim.

Something knocks on the outside of the car. Twice. Then from the next car forward. Then from beneath the floor.

Wheels checks the floor hatch: chalk wards on the tunnel wall, intact, in both directions, as far as the flashlight carries. This stretch decommissioned three months ago. No crew assigned.

The train resumes after 90 seconds without explanation.

*SAN: Knocking — 0/1 · Intact wards in darkness — 1/1D4*

---

**2-5: Union Square Cache (~15 min) ← DO NOT CUT**

Maintenance room off the Union Square transfer complex. Wheels locates it. Locked.

1. **Route Maps:** Subway lines annotated with dates. Circled stations align exactly with Spray's sketchbook wards.
2. **Ritual Timetable (H4):** "Summit / death of the anchor / gate opens at end of line / six hours to full manifestation." ANCHOR underlined three times. Cyrus was the anchor.
3. **Cassette Tape (H3):** Sony tape. "C — last argument." Working Walkman on the shelf.

> [Cyrus]: *"I'm not surrendering authority. I'm proposing a working arrangement."*
> [Creed/Conductor — distorted harmonic beneath]: *"You don't understand the thing below the rails, Cyrus. You don't lead it. You schedule it."*
> [Cyrus]: *"Then I'll schedule it."*
> [Creed/Conductor]: *"No. You'll be the lock. And we will find a key."*
> [Metal scraping. Long silence. Recording ends.]

After this scene the investigators know: (1) Cyrus's death was the ritual mechanism. (2) They were framed because of the token. (3) Deadline 6AM. After 7AM — no known procedure.

*Clues: route maps, ritual timetable (H4), cassette tape (H3)*
*SAN: Reading the timetable fully — 1/1D6*

---

**2-6: The Platform Freeze (~5 min)**

Normal late crowd. Then every person stops — INSTANTLY. Mid-step. Mid-sentence. Mid-reach.

Six seconds exactly.

A train arrives not on any schedule. Passes through without stopping. Every car lit, every car occupied, every passenger facing forward at exactly the same angle.

The stopped people resume. The investigators were the only ones who saw it.

*Count the six seconds out loud if you can manage it.*
*SAN: Platform freeze / ghost train — 1/1D6*

---

### ACT 3 — THE LAST TRAIN (~45 min)

**3-1: Brooklyn Approach (~12 min)**

Last workable southbound train. Three teenagers in prom wear in the adjacent car — untouched by the night. Rook looks at them and thinks about Marco. Name this moment.

**Creed's Parley:** At an unlit stop, Creed boards. Sets his revolver on his knee, pointed away. Offers to clear the Warriors' names in exchange for the token. Entirely credible. Lying about every element.

- **Psychology (Hard):** He is measuring, not negotiating.
- **Spot Hidden (Normal):** The revolver barrel is blackened. The round in the chamber carries the same tar residue as the summit shell casing.

*Play Creed calm, not menacing. Let the Spot Hidden do the menacing.*

---

**3-2: The Cult Strike (~15 min)**

Creed's team boards at the final stop — four Third Rail Choir enforcers plus Sister Vale. Goal: retrieve token, eliminate witnesses. Moving fight through connecting cars.

**Sister Vale:** Does not fight unless an enforcer dies in front of her. Isolated from Creed or exposed to the cassette tape: Persuade (Hard, Normal if cassette played). She is not certain the emergence is salvation. The tape is the crack Cyrus hoped someone would find.

*Keep the fight moving. Enforcers are competent, not supernatural.*

---

**3-3: Stillwell Terminal (~18 min)**

The maintenance rails have warped, over decades, into the Third Rail Choir sigil rendered in iron at the scale of a room. Power surges periodically. Black water on the tunnel floor conducts freely.

**The Breaker Box:** 1920s-era hardware. Shrine's anchor. Ritual objects going back thirty years. The relay at its centre is missing. One empty slot — machined to an unusual specification. The brass token fits it exactly. It was always meant to.

**The Black Conductor:** Does not arrive with drama. It is simply *there*. Tall figure assembled from cinders, ticket stubs, fraying wire, decades of soot — wearing the silhouette of a transit worker. Where a face should be, light bends away.

Voice from PA speakers, tunnel acoustics, and the investigators' own chests simultaneously:

*"This is the last stop. All passengers must exit here."*

**POW roll to place the token:**
- **Success:** Gate sealed. Electrical discharge incapacitates enforcers. Creed does not survive the arc. SAN cost: 1D8 permanent.
- **Failure:** Second investigator must assist (combined POW), or manifestation timeline advances 30 minutes.

*DO NOT DESCRIBE THE BLACK CONDUCTOR AT LENGTH. One sentence. Two at most. The accumulated dread is doing the work.*

*SAN: First full sight — 1D6/1D20 · Full-night exposure — 1D4/1D10 · Sealing the line — 1D8 PERMANENT · Taking the Bargain — 1D10*

---

### EPILOGUE — DAWN ON THE BEACH (15 min)

Address each investigator personally. Close their hooks.

**OUTCOME A — EXPOSE THE TRUTH:** The Riffs stand down. Masai hears the cassette. Gang justice turns on Creed. The Warriors walk the Coney Island boardwalk as the light comes up. Rook gets home before Marco wakes up.

**OUTCOME B — SEAL THE LINE:** The gate is sealed in a way that will never make the papers. The marked investigator does not tell the others what they felt when the power went through the token. Doc eventually learns the Riff runner was discharged from Kings County without record.

**OUTCOME C — THE LONG DEFEAT:** The investigators reach the beach. Every arriving train over the following days carries one more passenger than the manifest shows. Sandy Drucker stops sleeping and broadcasts around the clock. Marion Vale does not report for her transit shift and is not found for eleven days.

*Spend a sentence on each investigator's personal hook. Don't rush the epilogue.*

## PACING NOTES

- **Introduction → Act 1:** Do not let players deliberate. The city closes.
- **Act 1:** Establish dread through texture, not spectacle. The silver eyes. The matchbook. The dark car. Nothing explained.
- **Act 2:** The Union Square cache is the pivot of the entire scenario. Do not cut it. Everything before it is mystery. Everything after it is a race.
- **Act 3:** The Creed parley should feel like it might be the way out. The Spot Hidden on the revolver should close that door.
- **Stillwell:** Brief. The Black Conductor is not a speech-giver. One sentence of description. Let the investigators' accumulated dread do the rest.
- **Epilogue:** Fifteen minutes minimum. These characters earned it.

## GM NOTES

*Prepared for ChaosiumCon 2026 — Event 92*

The Warriors' run works as horror because the city's hostility is entirely ordinary — rival gangs, corrupt police, a transport system designed to contain and control — and the Mythos element is already woven through it. The players should feel like the supernatural is the *least* surprising thing about this night.

Cyrus is the emotional core. He died trying to save a city that would never have thanked him. The cassette tape is his message forward. Every scene that gets investigators closer to Stillwell is Cyrus's plan unfolding.

The Black Conductor does not want to destroy the city. It wants to run it. That is what makes it uncanny rather than simply monstrous.

**Key Keeper commitments:**
1. Never explain the silver eyes.
2. The cassette tape is the heart. Protect it narratively — make sure it reaches Act 2.
3. Stillwell: brief. One sentence on the Black Conductor. Silence does the rest.
