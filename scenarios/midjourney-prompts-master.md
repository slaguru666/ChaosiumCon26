# EVENT 92 — THE LAST TRAIN TO CONEY ISLAND
**Setting:** New York City, 1979. Six Warriors gang members framed for murder,
hunted across the city by every gang in the five boroughs. A Nyarlathotep entity
— the Black Conductor — has ridden the transit system since before the IRT line.
**Art style:** 1970s NYC grit, urban noir, ink wash, high contrast. Think
The Warriors meets early Frank Miller. Gritty pulp paperback aesthetic.
**10 prompts — ZERO art currently exists.**

---

### 92-ART-01 — COVER

```
Wide vertical composition. Six young people in gang vest colours sprinting through
a darkened 1970s New York City subway tunnel. The tunnel curves ahead of them into
black. Behind them at a great distance, barely visible: a tall pale figure in an
old conductor's uniform, standing completely motionless, face a featureless pale
oval. Neon light catches wet concrete and old station tile. Ink wash illustration,
high contrast, 1970s urban crime graphic novel style, not photorealistic, deep
blacks, Frank Miller influenced, dark atmospheric
--ar 2:3 --style raw
```

---

### 92-ART-02 — VAN CORTLANDT PARK: THE MURDER

```
Wide ink illustration of a midnight scene in an urban park, 1979. A crowd of
dozens of young people scatter in panic. At the centre of the composition, a tall
man in a long coat falls backward, arms wide, shot. In the foreground, a hand on
the ground with a warm brass token visible in the open palm. Park floodlights
cut out overhead, plunging most of the scene to shadow and fleeing figures.
1970s NYC, gritty ink wash, high contrast, reportage illustration style, crowd
movement suggested through broken lines and suggestion
--ar 4:3 --style raw
```

---

### 92-ART-03 — THE WARRIORS RUN: SUBWAY PLATFORM SPRINT

```
Six young New Yorkers in matching vest gang colours sprinting along a 1970s New
York City subway platform. Emergency lighting harsh overhead. Graffiti covers every
surface — layered aerosol tags, carved initials, hand-lettered murals. A subway
train to the right, doors just closing. Figures are mid-motion: some vaulting
turnstiles, one looking back. High contrast ink illustration, gritty 1970s urban,
no photorealism, dynamic kinetic composition
--ar 4:3 --style raw
```

### 92-ART-04 — THE PLATFORM REFLECTION VISION

```
A 1970s New York City subway platform at night, empty and dimly lit. In the dark

standing, packed, completely motionless, faces blank, going nowhere. The actual
car on the platform is empty, doors open, lights flickering. One figure stands on
the platform looking at the reflection. Perfect symmetry between real and reflected.
Ink illustration, uncanny atmosphere, high contrast blacks, supernatural horror in
a mundane transit setting, extremely quiet and wrong
--ar 4:3 --style raw
```

# EVENT 94 — THE CURIOUS CASE OF THE YORKSHIRE
**Setting:** Victorian Yorkshire, 1880s. Monstrous figure stalking the moors under
the full moon. Clawed footprints, torn livestock, a gentleman whose behaviour grows
increasingly strange.
**Art style:** Victorian Gothic illustration. Gustave Doré's dark engraving style,
Punch magazine horror serials, Arthur Rackham's darker work. Pen and ink,
crosshatching, deep shadow.
**10 prompts — ZERO art currently exists.**

---

### 94-ART-01 — COVER

```
Wide vertical Victorian pen-and-ink illustration of empty Yorkshire moorland under
a full moon. At the crest of a distant hill, a large dark silhouette — not quite
a man, not quite a beast, crouched and watching. In the foreground, a narrow stone
track leading away from the viewer into the moor. The moon is enormous. The shadows
are deep. Victorian engraving style, crosshatched blacks, Gustave Doré influenced,
Gothic atmosphere, no faces visible on the creature
--ar 2:3 --style raw
```

---

### 94-ART-02 — THE YORKSHIRE VILLAGE AT DUSK

```
A small Yorkshire stone village at dusk, 1880s. Cottages with low roofs, a church
spire, a narrow main street with gas lanterns just being lit by a lamplighter.
Several villagers are moving quickly indoors, one closing shutters. Windows glow
amber. The sky above the moor beyond the village is dark and unsettled. Everything
in the composition suggests the village pulling inward. Victorian pen and ink,
crosshatching, quiet dread, no monster visible
--ar 4:3 --style raw
```

### 94-ART-03 — THE DARK MOORS AT NIGHT

A wide Yorkshire moorland landscape at full dark. A narrow stone path cuts through

--ar 16:9 --style raw

### 94-ART-04 — THE CLAWED FOOTPRINTS

# STORMBRINGER — THE STORM OF KELEN'S PACT
**Setting:** Mael's Ford, Eastern Marches of the Young Kingdoms. A desperate
sorcerer's pact with a demon goes wrong. Six adventurers must reach the Spire
of Kyelm before the demon escapes its binding.
**Art style:** 1970s–80s fantasy illustration. Michael Whelan / John Blanche
register. Jewel tones bleeding into deep shadow. Storm-lit skies, crumbling
Melnibonéan stone, candlelit chambers.
**NOTE:** The generic Stormbringer art library exists (characters, creatures, etc.)
but there is NO specific scene art for this scenario.
**6 prompts.**

---

### SB-S01 — COVER: MAEL'S FORD UNDER CHAOS SKY

```
Wide panoramic fantasy illustration of a small stone village in the Eastern Marches
under a sky full of impossible storm colours — deep purple, bruised green, dark
crimson cloud — the Chaos stars visible at the edges of the storm. On a hill above
the village, the ruined silhouette of an ancient stone spire. Six adventurers on the
road leading toward the ruins, small against the landscape. 1970s–80s fantasy
illustration, Michael Whelan influenced, jewel tones into deep shadow, dark
sword-and-sorcery atmosphere, oil painting quality
--ar 2:3 --style raw
```

---

### SB-S02 — KELEN PORTRAIT: THE DESPERATE SORCERER

```
Portrait of a sorcerer in his middle years, wearing scholar's robes recently damaged
and hastily repaired. His face is tired and frightened — not the fear of combat but
the fear of a man who has made a grave mistake with no clean way back. He holds a
torn page of magical notation in one hand. Behind him, a stone chamber lit by a
single candle throwing long shadow. 1970s fantasy illustration, oil painting texture,
Michael Whelan inspired, candlelit, human and fallible — not a villain, a warning
--ar 3:4 --style raw
```

### SB-S03 — MAEL'S FORD: THE VILLAGE AT NIGHT

A small dark stone village in the Eastern Young Kingdoms, night. Narrow streets

notice board at the crossroads. The sky shows the Chaos stars — colours fractionally
shifted, moving too quickly. Villagers are absent but their absence is felt. 1970s–80s
fantasy illustration, dark palette, atmospheric, John Blanche influenced, brooding

Six sword-and-sorcery adventurers crossing a dark moorland at night beneath a sky

An ancient stone tower in ruin, standing on a low hill above the Eastern Marches

# EVENT 91 — BRP: THE NIGHT CRAWLER
**Setting:** Neo-Ashford, 2087. Corporate dystopia / Lovecraftian horror. A routine
retrieval job in the Undertow sub-districts becomes contact with a Spawn of the
Lurker at the Threshold.
**Art style:** Ink sketch + pencil illustration, high contrast, rain-slicked noir.
Blade Runner grit meets Lovecraftian dread.
**NOTE:** 5 NPC portraits already exist (Sable Morn, Damos Reth, Dr Yava Chen,
Jed Osler, Scratch). These 6 prompts cover ALL missing scene/cover art.

---

### 91-ART-01 — COVER: AERIAL NEO-ASHFORD AT NIGHT

```
Aerial composition looking down on a 2087 megacity at night. Eight arcology towers
impossibly tall rise above a permanent layer of industrial smog, their upper tiers
lit in corporate blue and white above the cloud line. Below the smog: a network of
amber and red neon from the street-level districts, rain-slick elevated roads,
mid-rise buildings tangled with wire. The street-level Undertow visible as dense
warm glow beneath everything. The scale is overwhelming — this is a machine humans
live inside. Ink sketch and pencil illustration, high contrast, rain-slicked noir,
Blade Runner influenced but hand-drawn not photographic, deep blacks
--ar 2:3 --style raw
```

---

### 91-ART-02 — THE SLAB: NEON MARKET, THE CHILD

```
An elevated road in a 2087 dystopian megacity, converted into a permanent covered
market. Cargo containers as storefronts, wire rigging overhead strung with neon
signs, crowded with people in practical layered clothes, filter scarves, visible
street augments. Slightly caustic rain falls. At the centre of the composition, a
small child — nine years old — presses a folded note into the outstretched hand of
an adult whose back is to the viewer. The child's face is urgent. The crowd moves
around them, not watching. Ink sketch and pencil, neon noir, high contrast
--ar 4:3 --style raw
```

---

### 91-ART-03 — RETH'S SQUAT: THE CORKBOARD

```
Interior of a cheap rented room in a 2087 corporate dystopia — small, cramped, the
air of someone frightened in a small space. On the wall: a corkboard covered in
handwritten paper notes. The handwriting starts neat at the top and becomes erratic
and pressing at the bottom. A hand-drawn map of sub-level infrastructure with one
route marked in red. At the very bottom of the corkboard, pinned crookedly: a
photograph, partially obscured, in which something behind the nominal subject refuses
to resolve into any familiar shape. The photograph is the focus. Ink sketch,
dystopian interior, compressed tension
--ar 4:3 --style raw
```

---

### 91-ART-04 — THE JUNCTION: BIOLUMINESCENT FILAMENT GROWTH

```
A large circular drainage hub 400 metres below a 2087 megacity. Six tunnel mouths
converge on the space. The walls, ceiling, and concrete support columns are covered
in a pale filamentous organic growth — faintly bioluminescent, warm to the touch,
not fungal, not any known taxonomy. The faint light it gives is not enough to see
by but enough to know it is there. The space has a quality of attention — not
emptiness. Six human figures tiny in the lower frame, their own lights carving pale
cones. Ink sketch and pencil illustration, clinical organic body horror, vast
underground space, Lovecraftian dread without gore
--ar 4:3 --style raw
```

---

### 91-ART-05 — THE THREE BODIES: THE FEED CORRIDOR

```
A long tunnel in 2087 sub-level infrastructure, walls thick with pale organic
filament growth, ceiling low. On the tunnel floor: three sets of remains in
decomposed corporate security gear. Each torso has been opened — not violently, but
with geometric organic precision. The ribcages are splayed outward like flowers in
full bloom, each rib bent at the joint with careful logic. No blood. The chest
cavities are empty and clean. The composition is deliberately cold and clinical —
viewed from a distance. The horror is in the mechanism, not the gore. Ink sketch,
forensic observation, high contrast, Lovecraftian body horror through restraint
--ar 4:3 --style raw
```

---

### 91-ART-06 — THE PRIMARY NODE: THROUGH THE GALLERY GLASS

```
View through reinforced gallery glass into the sub-level chamber below. Beyond the
glass: an entity approximately the size of a transit vehicle. Radially symmetrical
in a way that maps onto no known biology. Moving in constant slow motion with the
quality of a tide or a thought. It does not have eyes. The sense that it has
perceived the viewer looking at it is overwhelming. On this side of the glass: the
lower half of a single figure standing at the window, dwarfed. Ink sketch and pencil,
the Lovecraftian entity rendered through restraint — the shape should resist
comprehension, high contrast, clinical framing
--ar 4:3 --style raw
```

# EVENT 95 — RINGWORLD: A QUESTION OF SINGULARITY
**Setting:** The Ringworld, a circular quiet zone. A distributed management system
has been running a gravitational loop for 300 years to correct a structural
inconsistency, and the crew of the Foolish Endeavour are trapped inside it.
**Art style:** 1984 Larry Niven SF paperback. Ink + pencil, clean linework, vast
spaces, Ringworld-scale engineering.
**NOTE:** 7 SVG illustrations exist but are computer-generated. These 6 prompts
give Midjourney equivalents for the most important scenes, plus new ones.

---

### 95-ART-08 — MARR PORTRAIT: THE OBSESSED SCIENTIST

```
Portrait of a human scientist in their late 50s, wearing practical off-world survey
gear that has not been properly maintained in some time. They sit among hundreds of
stacked and scattered notebooks — floor to ceiling, surrounding them on all sides.
Their expression is the particular look of someone who has been thinking about one
problem far too long: exhausted, certain, and slightly lost. Ink and pencil, 1984
science fiction paperback style, cross-hatching for shadow, the notebooks making
the isolation absolute
--ar 3:4 --style raw
```

---

sleek but purposeful. The crew is clearly small against where they are. Ink and
pencil, 1984 SF group illustration, clean linework, Ringworld scale implied
--ar 4:3 --style raw
```

---

### 95-ART-10 — THE LOOP RESET: GRAVITY INVERTS

```
An alien Ringworld plain, ordinary in every respect — heather-analogue, flat ground,

mid-motion, suspended at the instant gravity inverts. Some mid-step, one mid-fall,

--ar 4:3 --style raw
```

The Foolish Endeavour — a compact angular 1984-aesthetic science vessel — on the

Open notebooks spread across the floor of a scientist's shelter on an alien plain.

them. Ink illustration, document close-study, archeological evidence of a mind
under impossible strain

```
