## MASTER RULES (apply to all scenarios)

### Structure
- **Introduction** → **Act One** → **Act Two** → **Act Three** → **Epilogue**
- Maximum runtime: **3 hours 30 minutes (210 minutes)**
- Acts are weighted: Act One ~30%, Act Two ~45%, Act Three ~15%, Intro/Epilogue ~10%
- Act Three **always** happens — compress Act Two if needed, never sacrifice the climax

### Clues
- Clues follow a **guided trail** — each clue points toward the next
- The trail should not be obvious; players should feel they discovered it, not followed a map
- Every act has at least **one essential clue** (must be found) and **one optional clue** (rewards thorough players)
- If players miss an optional clue, the trail remains intact
- If players miss an essential clue, the GM has a fallback (NPC volunteer, environmental prompt)

### NPCs
- Every NPC has: physical description, motivation, what they know, disposition, and stat block
- Stat blocks are system-specific and noted with a quick-reference tag (see Mechanics)

### System Mechanics
- Mechanics are called out inline as quick references only
- Format: `[MECHANIC: Skill/Roll — what it reveals or does]`
- Examples:
  - `[CoC: Spot Hidden — notices the torn hem]`
  - `[CoC: Sanity 0/1D4 — witnessing the creature]`
  - `[BRP: Perception — hears movement upstairs]`
  - `[Ringworld: Awareness — detects energy signature]`

### Artwork
- Style: **ink sketch and pencil illustration**
- Feel: hand-drawn, simple, not photorealistic
- Each scenario defines its own specific art direction
- Assets are generated per scenario and listed in the Artwork section
- Asset IDs follow format: `[EventNum]-ART-[##]` (e.g., `95-ART-01`)
