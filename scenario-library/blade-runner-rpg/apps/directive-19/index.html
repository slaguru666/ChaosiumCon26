⚠ The Truth — GM Only
Open before the session, not at the table
▼

In 2027, between the Blackout and the Wallace monopoly, a covert City Hall cell ran Directive 19 — three conscripted Nexus-8 combat replicants executed seven political and corporate enemies blocking Wallace's path to the replicant manufacturing licence.

The architect was Helena Cortez, then a 30-year-old aide, now Deputy Mayor and mayoral frontrunner. The handlers included Captain Voss (Cortez's security), Tyrell lawyer Hadley Brent (deceased 2031), Detective Aaron Ostrow (bought, not killed; killed in 2037 by Wren), and Elias Pham (Tyrell sec director, hiding at the marina).

After the seven targets were dead, the three replicants — Kira, Nine, and Wren — were taken to the Saltgrove warehouse to be decommissioned. Kira and Nine were executed and dissolved. Wren escaped.

She spent ten years preparing. Three weeks ago she returned and began killing the handlers. She has trained a human apprentice — Sera Park, daughter of Helen Park (journalist, victim no. 5).

Wallace has dispatched a Nexus-9 cleaner — Halden — to retire Wren and shut the file. He will kill anyone who gets in the way.

By design, Wren escapes at the end. The terrace scene determines what she costs the PCs — not whether she survives.

GM Notes — Critical Reminders
Read on prep day
▼

Tone: Slow Act One. Don't fill silences. Let players assemble it.

Halden restraint: Use him 3 times max. Polite, polite, polite, kills. Never engage directly unless PCs overwhelm.

Wren in Act Two: She does not engage. She kills Pham, says her line, leaves through a third-storey window.

Cortez: Not a cartoon. She believes she was right. Give her one private grace before the session.

Personal jeopardy: Pick the PC and the dead contact before the session. Don't improvise this on the night.

Terrace scene: Read Wren's lines slowly. Don't let dice decide whether she lives — they decide what she costs.

2:45 hard deadline: Team must be moving toward the Sandoval Tower. If not, Greer orders them home — they go off the books.
