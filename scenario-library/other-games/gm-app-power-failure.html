⚠GM TONE NOTES — BEFORE YOU BEGIN
The horror is layered. The entity is real, but it isn't the worst thing here. SLA Industries created the conditions and will bury the squad if needed. Reward player paranoia.

The D-Notice is the first red flag. Blue BPNs don't come with D-Notices. Let that land.

The rain never stops on Mort. Every exterior scene: mention it. Inside, they can still hear it on the roof. Note when it becomes inaudible — they've gone too deep.
