1
Primary Objective

Operatives are tasked to investigate and secure the abandoned Keller-Voss Energy Generation Plant, Downtown District 2, Sector 18. Determine the cause of local power fluctuations and civilian disappearances in surrounding residential Blocks 47–52. Restore facility to safe shutdown status or submit formal demolition recommendation to issuing department.

2
Operational Background

The Keller-Voss Energy Generation Plant has been offline for 14 months following completion of routine decommissioning procedures. All shutdown documentation was filed and accepted by the Department of Infrastructure & Energy on the recorded decommissioning date.

Recent monitoring data indicates unexplained power surges and fluctuations affecting residential supply across Blocks 47–52, consistent with a source within or adjacent to the Keller-Voss site. Local Shiver Division patrols (Sector 18, Downtown District 2) have additionally reported unauthorised access activity, possible squatter occupation, and civilian disappearances in the immediate vicinity of the facility perimeter over the past three weeks. Twelve civilians have been reported missing; investigations remain ongoing.

Site Observations — Shiver Reports
Unauthorised perimeter access — main gate compromised
Possible squatter activity reported, lower levels
Intermittent electrical discharge observed from exterior
Twelve civilian disappearances, Blocks 47–52 (3 week period)
Perimeter declared exclusion zone — Shiver cordon active
Expected Scope of Work
Secure and clear all access points and levels
Identify and eliminate cause of power anomalies
Locate and recover any surviving personnel
Assess structural integrity for shutdown / demolition
Submit full incident report to issuing department on completion
3
Prior Operative Activity — Incident 4478-A

An Operative squad (internal designation: Hammer-6, SCL 9) was assigned to an earlier iteration of this BPN and entered the facility six days prior to the issue of this document. Of the six Operatives assigned, five are currently listed as KIA or MIA. The sixth was extracted by trauma response and is currently on indefinite medical leave. The threat assessment for this BPN was revised from LOW to MEDIUM-HIGH following review of Incident 4478-A.

Operatives accepting this BPN are advised that prior squad equipment and personal effects may be present within the facility and are subject to the salvage rights clause of this assignment.
