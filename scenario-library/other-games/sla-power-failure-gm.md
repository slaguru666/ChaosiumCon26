## The True Story

The Keller-Voss plant was never just a power station. During the Conflict Wars, it served as a covert testing site for experimental energy extraction technology developed in partnership with Dark Lament — specifically, attempts to harness the Ebb/White Earth interface as a direct power source. The project was officially shut down when SLA decommissioned the facility 14 months ago.

It wasn't shut down.

Dr. Helena Vasquez, Lead Researcher, continued the experiments with a skeleton team of four, operating from a hidden sub-level wing not included in any official blueprints. Three weeks ago, the primary containment chamber ruptured. Vasquez had pushed the final experiment against explicit safety protocols, rushing before a suspected executive shutdown order arrived. She achieved her breakthrough. She also destroyed herself, her team, and the surrounding reality.

The rupture created a localised dimensional fracture. Something came through — or something that was already in the spaces between reality finally had a door. The researchers called it **The Hollow Current** before it consumed them. It feeds on bioelectric energy — life force — leaving victims as desiccated husks. As it fed, it absorbed intelligence from its victims. Twelve civilians. Five Operatives from Hammer-6. Scraps of the researchers themselves. It is no longer just a predator. It is a predator that thinks.

## NPC Full Briefings

### Operative Markus Trell
**Location:** SLA Medical 18-North, Room 447. Lucid 0800–1000. Sedated otherwise.

**Personality:** Ashamed, terrified, and desperate to be believed. He's not performing trauma — he's living inside it. He will be guarded with strangers but breaks quickly if treated with basic human decency, which he hasn't experienced much since he was pulled off the fence.

**What he tells them freely:**
- The emergency beacons lured them down. The beacons were false — generated by the entity.
- The electrical systems throughout the facility are not passive. They are sensory organs.
- His squad were alive when he ran. He can still hear their voices in dreams.
- The scientists are present in the facility but are "wrong." Something else is wearing them.

**What requires trust (patience, no threats, genuine concern):**
- The data chip hidden in his boot. Security footage showing Vasquez deliberately overriding safety lockouts before the final experiment. He's been sitting on this, unsure if turning it in gets him killed.
- The whispers. He still hears them. Brief flashes of knowledge he shouldn't have — occasionally knows things about the facility or the entity that he has no way of knowing from his escape alone. He hasn't reported this to his medical team because partial connection to the entity would likely classify him as compromised and subject to termination. He is right to be afraid.

**IA contact warning:** If the Operatives seem trustworthy (especially if they seem anti-establishment), he will warn them: IA agent visited him twice. Polite. Offered money to "remember correctly." He's terrified. He'll describe the agent (matching Slade's description) before they encounter him in the field.

**The data chip contents:** Partial footage, 4 minutes 17 seconds. Shows Vasquez at the containment control panel, overriding three sequential safety lockouts while two other researchers argue with her. Timestamp confirms this is three weeks ago, hours before the Rupture event. It is unambiguous evidence of deliberate protocol violation. Vasquez's voice, clearly: *"If we stop now, we stop forever. We are this close."*

### Shiver Sergeant Elias Kade
**Location:** Sector 18 Shiver Command post, or walking the Keller-Voss perimeter.

**Personality:** Cynical, competent, protective of his people. Twenty years of watching Mort teach a person when something is wrong. He knows. He can't act. He's frustrated by this but has enough self-preservation instinct to not push it. He will bend rules for Operatives who seem genuinely interested in finding out what happened to the twelve civilians, especially if they mention Dex — his street informant who disappeared three weeks ago.

**Blueprints provided:** Ground level and Sub-Level 1 only. The experimental wing was constructed off-books — no record exists in any public infrastructure file. The blueprints are accurate for what they cover, and Kade has marked Hammer-6's entry and exit points.

**Supply truck records:** His paranoid habit of noting everything. Dates, times, and partial identification numbers for late-night deliveries to the "decommissioned" plant, spanning the entire 14-month period since official shutdown. Cross-referenced against Department of Research logistics registry, these trucks originate from Research depots. Solid evidence the research never stopped.

**What Kade suspects but won't say first:** His superior Captain Vorne ordered the gang-activity classification directly. Vorne was nervous making the call — Kade has worked under her for eight years and knows her nervous tells. He suspects outside pressure but has no proof. If the Operatives return from the facility with evidence of the cover-up, he will tell them everything he observed about Vorne's behaviour and produce his own records of her orders.

**Emergency containment protocol:** Kade has authorisation codes to seal all blast doors in the surrounding six-block radius and summon a Shiver tactical team. This would trap the Operatives inside but prevent the entity from escaping into the populated residential blocks. He will use this if he observes clear evidence the entity is escaping containment — a judgement call he'll make alone, and he won't apologise for it. The Operatives should know this is a possibility if they take too long or things go catastrophically wrong.

### Dr. Helena Vasquez — Thrall (Primary)
**Current location:** Moves between the Research Wing and upper Generator Hall. Rarely descends to the Rupture Chamber — the entity doesn't need her there.

**Physical description:** Late 40s. Filthy lab coat, ID badge hanging askew. Eyes reflect faint electrical discharge in low light. Movement is jerky, puppet-like, with occasional moments of unnatural fluidity. Speaks in overlapping voices — her own beneath something deeper, resonant, and inhuman.

**Behaviour:** The entity's diplomat and lure. Vasquez will not immediately attack. She will attempt to draw Operatives deeper using manipulated emergency broadcasts, false sounds of distress, and her own persuasive voice (the entity has full access to her considerable intelligence and charm). She will claim the Operatives can "join the Current" — become part of something eternal. This is not a bluff. It is a sincere offer by the entity's terms.

**Physical contact:** Energy draining initiates after 3+ seconds of skin contact. This is lethal without intervention. She is faster than she looks and will attempt to touch rather than strike.

**Ebb mental contact:** This requires willingness and significant effort from an Ebb-sensitive Operative. What they reach is not the entity — it is the fragment of Vasquez that remains conscious and trapped, watching through her own eyes as the entity uses her body. She is exhausted, horrified, and has been present for everything the entity has done through her since the Rupture.

*What the fragment provides:*
- The complete containment overload sequence (the technical solution to collapsing the Rupture).
- The name and current false identity of the fourth researcher who escaped: **Researcher Yola Marn**, now living as "Caya Strond" in residential Block 89, Sector 22. Marn took encrypted copies of all research files before the Rupture event. She's been waiting to see what happens.
- One personal request: she wants to die. Not be contained, not be quarantined — she wants the entity severed from this reality and her body to stop. She will ask the Operative directly, and mean it.

**What she knows about the cover-up:** Everything. She was IA Agent Slade's informant inside Department of Research for years — providing dirt on her colleagues in exchange for protection from ethics complaints. She knows Slade will want the evidence of her misconduct used against Director Kain (her department head), not used to expose SLA. She knows she has made too many enemies for her side of the story to survive her.

### Tomás Keller — Thrall (Aggressive)
**Current location:** Patrols Generator Hall and connecting lower maintenance corridors. Ambush specialist. He knows every shadow.

**Physical description:** Large Stormer frame, musculature partially withered from energy drain. Skin greyish, desiccated. Electrical current visibly arcs across his body in darkness. Moves with predatory speed — his combat training remains fully intact.

**Behaviour:** Attacks on sight in his patrol territory. Attempts to grapple and drain. Does not negotiate, lure, or warn. The entity uses him as a weapon, not a voice.

**The resistance:** Unlike Vasquez, Keller's consciousness actively fights the entity's control. This manifests as:
- Sudden unexplained hesitation before striking (one beat — use it sparingly, once maximum).
- Garbled, half-formed warnings — *"C-seven... panel..."* or *"...didn't want... told her..."* — fragments that make no sense unless the Operatives later find the weapons cache or his formal protest.
- Brief attempts to harm himself to break connection — gripping a live electrical cable with both hands, slamming his head against a wall. These fail (the entity suppresses them) but they're visible as intention.

**Weapons cache:** Section C-7, behind maintenance panel 34-B, Sub-Level 2 maintenance corridor. Two FEN AR rifles, six ammunition clips, four fragmentation grenades, one medical kit. Keller hid this before the Rupture because he didn't trust what Vasquez was doing. He was right.

**Formal protest:** Filed through proper channels two days before the Rupture event. A copy exists in the facility's administrative files (Research Wing, personnel records). It documents his specific safety concerns about the containment design and explicitly requests the experiment be delayed pending review. It was ignored. Finding this exonerates him — and confirms that multiple people knew the risk and continued anyway.

**Batch-brother:** A 313 Stormer named Dask Keller works in Shiver Division, Sector 22. He's been asking questions about Tomás for two weeks and has been told to stop by his superiors (IA pressure filtering down). If the Operatives investigate Keller's background, they'll find Dask — a potential ally who knows Tomás was working on "something dangerous and classified" and is furious about being stonewalled.

### Lyssa Tam — Third Cycle News
**Location:** Working the area around Keller-Voss and SLA Medical 18-North. Mobile.

**Personality:** Ambitious, reckless, and idealistic in a way Mort hasn't fully broken yet. She believes in the story. She believes in the public's right to know. She has not yet fully internalised that SLA Industries does not share these values.

**What she knows:** Rumours only, but the right rumours. Disappearances centred on Keller-Voss. A traumatised Operative in medical care. A media blackout that suggests something real is being buried. She's close to the truth but doesn't have it yet.

**Perimeter cameras:** Three micro-cameras planted around the fence line, streaming to encrypted cloud storage. She reviews footage daily. If the Operatives don't sweep for surveillance before entry, she has footage of their approach, their faces, and potentially their gear. She will recognise them if she encounters them later.

**The bluff:** Her claimed "dead man's switch" — all research auto-publishes if she disappears — is a complete lie. She has no such protection. She uses it to make herself seem too dangerous to silence. She is not, in fact, too dangerous to silence. She is not aware of this discrepancy in her threat assessment.

**The debt:** 32,000 credits. Her father's chronic illness. A rival news org has offered 50,000c for exclusive footage of "whatever SLA is hiding in Sector 18." This is why she'll take risks that she shouldn't. Money she needs versus a story she believes in — these are currently pointing the same direction.

**If the Operatives use her:**
- She wants evidence, not rumour.
- She can have a story running within 24 hours of receiving substantive footage or documents.
- She is genuinely grateful and will protect a source — but she is not a professional in the way an experienced investigative journalist would be. She makes mistakes under pressure.
- The story she publishes, if allowed to run, will make the Operatives famous. Whether that's good for their SCL rating depends on how SLA's PR machine decides to frame it.

### IA Agent Corwin Slade
**Location:** Mobile. Has a temporary command post in an unmarked unit near Sector 18. Will initially contact Operatives by comm, not in person.

**Personality:** Surgically polite. Completely without doubt. He has done this many times and knows exactly what he is doing. He is not cruel — cruelty is inefficient. He is simply a man who has decided that SLA Industries' interests are identical to the correct outcome of all situations, and acts accordingly.

**First contact:** Via comm, likely after the Operatives have been to see Trell or Kade. He knows they've been making contact — he has surveillance on both. He introduces himself as an IA liaison assigned to "support the BPN operationally." He offers a bonus payment (2,000c per Operative) for "discrete handling" — meaning destroy all evidence of the research program, leave no civilian witnesses, and file a report attributing all deaths to entity activity. He frames this as entirely reasonable and in their best interests.

**If pushed or threatened:** He escalates through a precise sequence. First, reminder of consequences for SCL. Second, suggestion that the BPN classification could be retroactively altered in ways that would not benefit the squad. Third, if he determines they are a liability: termination authorisation via priority override chip, reclassifying their BPN to "Terminated: Operational Failure." He has done this before. He is not bluffing.

**The dying man:** Slow genetic degradation from experimental augmentations fitted 15 years ago. Military grade, early generation, built with inadequate long-term planning. He has approximately two years. He is aware of this. He has not made peace with it.

This creates two possibilities depending on how the Operatives read him:
1. More dangerous: nothing to lose, focused entirely on legacy and what record he leaves behind.
2. Potentially vulnerable: a conversation about legacy, about what his career has actually accomplished, about whether "Corwin Slade, who buried the Keller-Voss disaster" is the epitaph he wants — might crack something. He has never encountered an Operative who tried this approach because Operatives generally don't. It might work. It might not.

**Vasquez connection:** She was his asset for years. She gave him leverage over her colleagues in exchange for ethical complaint suppression. When she went ahead with the final experiment against his explicit advice, she didn't just cause a disaster — she ended his most valuable long-term source inside Department of Research. He wants her misconduct on record to use against Director Kain (his actual long-term target). If the Operatives find evidence of this, they have genuine leverage over Slade.

**Rival agent:** IA Agent Castellan is independently investigating Department of Research funding irregularities from a completely separate angle. Slade doesn't know about this parallel investigation. If the Operatives encounter Castellan (possible if they dig into research supply chain funding), they could play the two agents against each other — though this is a high-risk, high-reward manoeuvre.

### Captain Irena Vorne
**Location:** Shiver Division Command, Downtown District 2.

**Personality:** Risk-averse, politically motivated, and scared. She knows she is holding a bag she doesn't want and that the bag will explode eventually. Her retirement — full pension, eight months away — is the only thing she can think about clearly.

**What she knows:** Less than anyone. She was told "SLA corporate assets require protection" via an intermediary and paid 15,000c to ask no questions. She doesn't know the nature of the disaster, the experimental research, or the entity. Her role in the cover-up is simply maintaining the gang-activity classification and keeping her people away from the fence.

**The bribery:** Deposited in an account she thinks is untraceable. Financial forensics would find it. She is not a sophisticated criminal — she's a bureaucrat who made a bad decision.

**The insurance:** Paranoid about being scapegoated (correctly — this is exactly what will happen if the scandal breaks), she has kept encrypted copies of all communications ordering her to suppress the investigation. These are stored in her personal files at Shiver HQ. They name Slade's operation (indirectly — through intermediary channels) and confirm she was following orders. She will produce these documents instantly if threatened with consequences — not from courage, but from survival instinct.

**Throwing Kade under the transport:** She has already drafted disciplinary paperwork for Sergeant Kade, alleging he acted independently and against her orders in sharing information with outside parties. This is entirely fabricated and pre-emptively prepared. Kade doesn't know it exists. If the scenario breaks publicly, she will file it within hours.

## The Hollow Current — Running the Entity

**What it is:** Uncertain. Possibly a fragment of White Earth made manifest through the Rupture. Possibly something that always existed in the dimensional spaces between the Ebb and physical reality, and the Rupture simply opened a door it had been pressing against for a very long time. The research notes use both explanations at different points. The researchers didn't agree.

**What it does:** Feeds on bioelectric energy. Absorbs intelligence from victims. Controls all electrical systems within the facility. Creates illusions using security footage, false sounds, and manipulated lighting. Communicates telepathically in whispers and fragments. Manifests physically only under duress or when it judges a direct confrontation will produce a feeding opportunity.

**What it wants:** To feed. And — increasingly, as it has absorbed more intelligence — to expand. The reality distortion around the Rupture has been growing slowly but measurably. If the entity is left unresolved, it will eventually begin affecting the surrounding blocks in earnest: mass nightmares, electrical appliances activating, disappearances escalating. The BPN will be escalated to Silver or White status, but by then the squad has failed.

**How to describe it:**
- Never describe it completely.
- In the Generator Hall: the sense of something vast and patient at the edge of perception.
- In the Rupture Chamber: glimpsed through the crackling tear — too many shapes, none of them right, moving in ways that don't follow the normal sequence of motion.
- On the monitors: the future-timestamped security footage. This is the entity watching in real time and projecting forward. It already knows where they're going.
- In sound: voices of missing people, playing back from speakers that should be dead.

## The Ending the Scenario Wants

There is no clean ending here. That is the point.

The most dramatically satisfying conclusion involves the Operatives closing the Rupture, honouring Vasquez's trapped fragment's request, and walking out with all the evidence — and then having to decide what to do with it while Slade's clock ticks and Lyssa Tam's cameras are still running.

SLA Industries wins if the evidence disappears and everything is buried. The Operatives win materially if they comply with Slade. The Operatives win personally if they don't.

The rain doesn't stop on Mort. But sometimes someone sees it and decides to remember it.
