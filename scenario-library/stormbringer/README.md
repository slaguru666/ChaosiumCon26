## About Stormbringer

Dark sword-and-sorcery fantasy. The Young Kingdoms are a world in decline, where Law and Chaos struggle for supremacy and ancient pacts with demon lords shape the fate of mortals.

Editions in use: Stormbringer 1st–5th, Elric! (1993), Magic World.
