## Notes for generation

- All six should feel like they come from the **same artist** — use the same 
  style words across all prompts for consistency
- If a prompt skews too polished or digital, add: 
  `--no photorealistic, --no CGI, --no digital art, --no 3D`
- Target **black and white with grey wash** — not full-colour, not pure line
- The final slot is **square (1:1)**, cropped at the shoulders or just below
- Once generated, drop the six image files into the repo and the build script 
  will pick them up from the paths in the character data dictionary
