## Usage Notes

- All images should be **black and white / monochrome** to match the 1984 paperback aesthetic
- Add `--no colour, --no photorealistic, --no 3D render, --no digital art` to any that skew too polished
- Target size: **42mm × 40mm print slot** (approximately 498×472px at 300dpi for print, or 630×600px at 150dpi for screen preview)
- Each portrait should be **cropped to head and shoulders** for the character sheet slot
- The finished art should feel like a **period illustration** — ink line art with pencil shading, not painted
