THE MORAL CHOICE
Three options. No clean answer.
Act III▼

PRESENT ALL THREE WITH EQUAL WEIGHT. Do not telegraph a preferred outcome.

Option 1 — Walk Away

Take the money. Sign the NDA. Say nothing. Entity remains imperfectly contained. Breaches final barriers in ~6 months. Secondary node in Sub-District 4 established by then.

Cost: The team knows. They will always know.

Option 2 — Expose Veltris

Use chip data to go public or to a rival corp. Veltris takes the damage. Programme shut down. Containment systems go offline as no one maintains them. Entity begins slow spread again with no one watching.

Cost: Corporation damaged. Entity released. Nobody knows what they're dealing with.

Option 3 — Destroy the Plant

Tech triggers catastrophic power cell overload. 10 minutes to rig. During those 10 minutes: barriers fail as power rerouted. Entity free in Sub-Level 9 — cannot reach upper floors in time. Explosion destroys primary node. Filaments wither over weeks. Secondary node in Sub-D4 unaffected.

Cost: Power/water/heat/medical to surrounding blocks goes out. ~15–30 civilian deaths. Four will be children.

If players try all three: the maths don't work. 10 min to rig + Reth + firefight upstairs exceeds any safe timeline. Two of three. Not all three.

The Entity — The Night Crawler

Nature

Spawn of the Lurker at the Threshold. Semi-corporeal. Predates Neo-Ashford by centuries. Distributed filament network IS the entity. Primary node is its densest concentration.

Sensory System

Perceives through pressure differential, heat, and "intent" — the quality of directed attention in living beings. It registered the team's approach before they entered Sub-District 7. When the team looks at it, it perceives the looking.

Combat

Cannot be conventionally killed. Primary node: destroyed by Tech's overload, 200+ HP damage, or equivalent explosive. Cutting filaments: AP 2, HP 8 per metre-section — slows it, draws network attention to that location in 1D4 rounds. Does not chase in a straight line — anticipates through the tunnel network.

Influence Mechanic

60+ cumulative minutes in tunnels: POW resistance vs POW 18. Fail: quiet pull toward lower levels — returning feels natural. Tell failing player PRIVATELY. Not mind control. Deep, slow environmental conditioning. How two of Scratch's people went back.
