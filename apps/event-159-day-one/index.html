The Truth (GM Only)
Meridian Biosciences / COBR decision
▼

Meridian Biosciences — MoD contractor, Elephant & Castle railway arches. Compound MX-9: neurological disruption agent. Containment breach Thursday 14 May.

Government knew by 8:30 AM. COBR convened 9:00 AM. Alert delayed until 10:47 — containment-first assessment.

The cordon north of the river is not rescue. It is containment. They are closing the door on South London.

Players don't need to know this to survive. Priya and Helen can piece it together. Let them pull the thread.

Introduction ~15 min
Run a 30-second snapshot for each character. Normal Sunday. Then every phone buzzes. Use the 📱 ALERT button in the header for the NHS alert reveal.
Individual Openings
Read aloud to each player in turn
▼

KIRA — Corner table, coffee shop near Tate Modern. Friend running late. Three NHS group chat messages unread. Outside, a cyclist clips a pedestrian. Normal Sunday.

DEV — Laptop open, Borough Market café, headphones half-on. Wi-Fi slow. Flatmate sent a voice note an hour ago asking about spare keys. Haven't replied.

MAGGIE — Jubilee Walkway, river on the left. Every Sunday, whatever the weather. 22 years on the force. She knows this stretch better than her own sitting room. Everything exactly as it should be.

OLU — Clocking in at the Shard lobby. Sunday overtime. The radio on the security desk isn't working properly. Last of a coffee brought from home. Normal Sunday.

PRIYA — Southbank Centre media centre. Science supplement piece due Tuesday. Editor's message last night, read and filed: "Something came in. Might be relevant. Can we talk Monday?"

TOM — In the van, parked off Waterloo Road. Final equipment check before the Sunday overtime scaffolding job. Radio on. Called his mum this morning. She didn't answer.

Every phone at the table buzzes simultaneously.

→ Fire the NHS Alert. Let it sit. Then: "Across the street, someone screams. It doesn't sound like shock. It sounds like pain."

Convergence Point
Getting the group together
▼

A bus has hit a parked delivery lorry on Southwark Street. Driver had a medical episode. Crowd of ~20 gathering. All on phones.

Among the crowd: one person standing very still, head tilted 15 degrees. Has not moved. This is the first infected. Not yet aggressive.

This is the gravity point that draws all six characters together.

Key Beats
The Blue Anchor Decision
The moral centre of the scenario
▼

DANNY — 30s, sitting in the corner, jacket pulled over his arm. Bitten 40 minutes ago. Hasn't told anyone. Deteriorating slowly.

Dr Sharif identifies him within 5 minutes if allowed to assess the group. Kira may also notice [BRP: Medicine/First Aid].

The group has ~30 minutes inside before Danny turns. The decision: warn the pub group (and risk panic), leave without saying anything, or try to quietly remove Danny.

SAN 1d2/1d8 — if Danny reanimates inside the pub with the group present.
The Reanimation
Act II/III hinge — MUST happen
▼

Choose the most significant NPC to the group. Recommended: Helen. Second: Danny in the pub.

If Helen: Separated briefly. Group turns. She is standing very still, 20 metres back. Head tilted. Hold the moment for four seconds before she moves.

If Danny: He stands up mid-conversation. Jacket falls. Wound visible. Pub survivors panic. Door blocked.

SAN 1d2/1d8 — witnessing reanimation of a known person.

Act III — The Bridge or the River ~30 min
Goal: Fast, physical, urgent. No new information — only consequences. Someone does not reach the exit.
The Two Routes
Tower Bridge
On foot — civilian group controls access
▼

PETE (50s, site foreman, fire axe) controls the gatehouse with 6–8 civilians. He is letting people through — after questioning. He will refuse anyone he believes is infected or lying.

He is not a villain. He is broken-steady. [BRP: Persuade — pass to move through. Psychology — reads his state: he has already turned two infected people away and dealt with the consequences.]

If Pete refuses someone: the group must choose. Leave them, or risk the bridge. No third option is provided. Players may invent one.

Bankside Pier — The RIB
Via Blackfriars underpass — heavily infected
▼

6-metre rigid inflatable at Bankside Pier. Holds 10. Key in locked pier office. Engine starts on third try.

Route requires the Blackfriars underpass — dark since noon, 12+ infected inside.

SAN 1d4/1d8 — moving through the underpass (darkness + density + moaning).

[BRP: Mechanics/Drive (Boat) — loud failure on startup draws infected from underpass within 3 minutes.]

Check the fuel gauge. Players must confirm fuel before trusting the escape route.

The Cost
THE COST — Required at 3:05
This must happen. Do not pull it.
▼

Someone does not make it. Choose:

Option A — An NPC: Helen, Fatima, Callum, or Marcus. Whichever has become most significant. Not a sacrifice — an accident. Random cruelty.

Option B — A PC: BRP is lethal. Low HP characters are at risk. Do not fudge rolls. Offer the player a final moment: a last instruction, something given to the group.

Option C — Moral choice: Pete refuses someone at the bridge (correctly). Leave them or risk everyone.

If nobody has died by 3:05: Option A. Choose Helen. Play it straight. Four seconds. Then she moves.

At 3:10 — bridge or boat reached. Cost paid. Move to Epilogue.

Technology (May 2026)
What works, what fails, what misleads
▼

Mobile phones: Primary info source. Social media contradicts itself every 30 seconds. Works for ~1 hour before 4G congestion collapses patchily.

Google Maps / Citymapper: Still functional hour 1. Streets are not what the map shows.

NHS Emergency Alert: Has fired. Every phone. Cannot be unseen.

TfL gates: Locked automatically in emergency mode. Traps people in stations.

AI assistants: Out-of-date results. Darkly comic. Ask aloud: "What is happening in London?" — get a story from three hours ago.

999: Hold music. Recorded message. Line drops.

Dockless Lime bikes: App still works longer than most. Useful transport.

Drone stream: Marcus or found independently. Bird's-eye view of the city. It is worse than expected.

Infection — The Arc
Does not resolve in this session
▼

Bite → CON roll. Fail: onset within hours. The arc is not resolved in this scenario.

The threat should hang over every bite taken in this session.

Visible symptoms: pallor, fever, disorientation, progressive slowing. Dr Sharif can identify at Medicine difficulty 40%.

Priya's editor may have Meridian Biosciences documents that describe MX-9 progression — available in a follow-up session.
